# Structured-thinking boundaries

The approved design is one portable skill at skills/structured-thinking/ with 25
method guides loaded as needed. Keep that explicit package path; this single general
toolkit does not justify a new bucket or belong exclusively to engineering.
It remains ungrouped in skills.sh.json and appears under Other skills.

The user subsequently approved 25 independently installable named method skills.
These also stay at skills/structured-thinking-*/ and remain ungrouped. Their self-contained
entrypoints are generated from the toolkit's canonical sources. This extends the original
single-package choice without introducing a host-specific command adapter or installer.

The first version excludes a custom CLI, an automated installer, agent-specific
command adapters, mandatory multi-method pipelines, and global installation.
Plain Markdown and documented host discovery paths cover the requested use.

Do not require an APPLY keyword, force analysis on routine edits, or treat a thinking
method as permission to execute its recommendations. Preserve the host's existing
rules and the user's task authorization.

Behavioral evaluations are a smoke sample. Several prompts resemble worked examples;
passing does not establish general reasoning improvement or broad host compatibility.
